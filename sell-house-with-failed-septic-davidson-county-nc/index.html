Seller Guides · Septic problems

Sell a House With a Failed Septic System in Davidson County, NC

You can ask for an offer before committing to a septic repair. Share whether the problem is a suspected backup, a documented failure, or a county notice. A buyer will want to know which solution the site can actually support.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Request the permit and any repair records

Ask Davidson County Environmental Health for available records on your parcel. An old permit, sketch, or repair evaluation can help show where the system and any repair area are located. Tell the buyer if the house has more rooms being used as bedrooms than the permit identifies. If records are missing, request guidance on the next step.

Get clarity on the repair before comparing offers

A pump failure and a failing drain field can involve very different work. Give the buyer any contractor report or county notice and ask whether the offer assumes a repair or a replacement. If the county has directed you to address a malfunction, confirm the required action with Environmental Health. The sale discussion and the county’s instructions both need attention.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Parcel number, permit or sketch, and the date symptoms started
  • Any inspection notes, repair estimates, or county notices
  • Whether the house is occupied and whether a well or other parcels share the site

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

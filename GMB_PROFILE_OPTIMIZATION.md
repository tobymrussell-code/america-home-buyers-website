## Questions And Answers To Seed

Google may allow owner-posted Q&A. Use plain helpful answers.

Q: Do I need to repair my house before requesting an offer?
A: No. America Home Buyers reviews properties in their current condition, including houses that need repairs, cleanout, or updates.

Q: Do I have to accept the offer?
A: No. Requesting an offer does not require you to sell. You can compare the direct-sale option with a traditional listing.

Q: What areas do you serve?
A: We focus on the North Carolina Triad, including Winston-Salem, Greensboro, High Point, Thomasville, Lexington, Kernersville, Archdale, Asheboro, and nearby communities.

Q: Can I sell an inherited house?
A: Yes. We can talk through inherited properties, belongings, repairs, timing, and whether a direct as-is sale may make sense.

Q: Can I leave belongings or debris behind?
A: Sometimes. Tell us what remains in the property so cleanout can be considered in the review and any agreement can be clear.

Q: How fast can you close?
A: It depends on title, access, payoff information, seller timing, and attorney scheduling. Cash can simplify the process, but the closing still needs to be handled correctly.

Describe the entire property before comparing options

For an Asheboro review, identify the house, any additional land and the buildings included in the sale. Explain known repairs, whether the house is occupied and whether utilities can be used safely. A direct offer needs to account for the whole scope of work and access.

If the property was inherited or is owned by more than one person, begin gathering ownership information early. Do not commit to a closing deadline until the people who must sign and the title requirements are understood.

Explore inherited property, houses needing repairs, tenant-occupied homes or liens and back taxes. Request a property review.

Downtown Asheboro and the Sunset Avenue area

The City of Asheboro places the historic Sunset Theatre on Sunset Avenue in downtown. Proximity to downtown is one part of a property review, but it does not replace checking the house’s condition, parking, access and permitted use. The city’s planning department provides zoning and downtown-development information when a proposed renovation or change of use needs clarification.

Trust checklist

Do this before choosing any cash home buyer.

A review page is useful, but it is not the whole decision. Make sure the buyer, terms, costs, and closing path are clear.

1

Read public reviews.

Look at Google reviews and whether the company appears reachable and local.

2

Ask how the offer works.

Make sure resale value, repairs, cleanout, holding costs, and risk are explained.

3

Review before signing.

Understand proof of funds, assignments, costs, deadlines, and what happens if the buyer does not close.

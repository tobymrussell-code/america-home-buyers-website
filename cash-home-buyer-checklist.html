Protect your decision

Questions to ask before signing with any cash home buyer.

A fast closing is only useful when you understand who is buying, what the agreement says, and what could change. Use this checklist with America Home Buyers or any competitor.

1

Who is actually buying the property?

Ask for the legal buyer name, company name, direct phone number, and the name of the person responsible for the transaction.

2

Are they the end buyer or assigning the contract?

Some investors buy directly and others may assign their contract to another buyer. Neither structure should be hidden. Read the assignment language and ask plainly.

3

Can they show credible proof of funds?

Ask for evidence that the buyer can complete the purchase. Protect sensitive financial documents and verify anything that appears unusual.

4

What inspections or due diligence remain?

Understand access requirements, inspection periods, cancellation rights, and whether the offer can be reduced after you sign.

5

Who pays which costs?

Ask about attorney fees, title work, liens, taxes, recording charges, commissions, cleanout, and any administrative or service fee.

6

What happens if the buyer does not close?

Review earnest money, deadlines, extensions, contingencies, default provisions, and your remedies with a North Carolina attorney.

7

Which attorney or closing office is involved?

Confirm the closing process and independently verify wiring or payoff instructions using a trusted phone number. Never rely only on an unexpected email.

8

Have you compared alternatives?

Consider another direct offer and an agent’s opinion of an as-is listing. The best path depends on price, condition, timing, privacy, and certainty.

Before you sign

Slow down long enough to understand the agreement.

Real estate contracts create legal obligations. This checklist is educational, not legal advice. Ask a North Carolina attorney to explain terms you do not understand.

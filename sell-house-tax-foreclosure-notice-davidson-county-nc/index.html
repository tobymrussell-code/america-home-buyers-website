Seller Guides · Tax foreclosure notice

Got a Tax Foreclosure Notice in Davidson County, NC?

Call the Davidson County Tax Collections Office and the contact on your notice first. Confirm the parcel, the current payoff, and whether an auction date has been set. If you’re considering a private sale, have a closing attorney confirm whether there’s enough time.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Ask for the payoff and the exact stage of the case

A past-due tax bill and a notice with an auction date call for different decisions. Ask which years are owed, what interest or costs are included, when the payoff expires, and who can confirm that the sale is canceled if the balance is resolved. Davidson County lists its Tax Collections Office at (336) 242-2181.

Compare paying the balance with selling before the auction

If keeping the house is your goal, ask the office what options are available in your case. If you want to sell, the closing attorney can review the payoff, other liens and title, and whether the transaction can close in time. An offer or signed contract by itself doesn’t stop an auction. A Thomasville family called us after receiving tax foreclosure notices and ultimately sold the property as-is. Read what happened on Unity Street.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Property address, parcel ID, and notice date
  • Any auction date or case number on the notice
  • Current payoff if the county has provided it and whether the house was inherited

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

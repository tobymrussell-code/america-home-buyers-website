## Priority 1: Must Do First

- Google Business Profile: already exists. Keep updating it weekly.
- Bing Places: claim or create the listing so Bing, Windows, Microsoft Copilot, and map partners can see it.
- Apple Business: claim the business so Apple Maps and Siri have accurate details.

## Priority 2: Trust Builders

- BBB business profile: get listed first; consider paid accreditation only if the cost makes sense.
- Nextdoor Business Page: helpful for local homeowner visibility.
- Alignable: local business network profile; useful if you connect with contractors, attorneys, property managers, agents, and investors.
- Chamber/local directory listings: Thomasville, Lexington/Davidson County, High Point, Greensboro, Winston-Salem, and Asheboro/Randolph County where affordable.

## Priority 3: Industry and Relationship Links

These are usually more valuable than random directories.

- Closing attorneys you work with: ask if they have a partner/vendor/resource page.
- Contractors and cleanout vendors: ask for a project partner mention.
- Local investor groups: profile, sponsor page, or member directory.
- Real estate agent and property manager relationships: ask for a resource-page link where appropriate.
- Local podcasts, newsletters, and community sites: contribute a short seller-education article.

2. Reply to any reviews.
3. Ask one real contact for a review.
4. Publish or draft one seller-answer article.
5. Build or update one citation/profile.
6. Record profile URL and login/status in the tracker.

## What Not To Do

- Do not buy fake reviews.
- Do not create dozens of low-quality spam directory listings at once.
- Do not publish a private address.
- Do not keyword-stuff the business name.
- Do not use different phone numbers unless call tracking is configured carefully.
- Do not claim awards, years in business, transaction counts, or rankings unless they are true and documented.

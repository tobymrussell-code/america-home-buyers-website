Privacy Policy

America Home Buyers LLC respects the privacy of property owners who contact us. This policy explains the information collected through this website and how it may be used.

Information you provide

When you submit a property offer form, you may provide your name, email address, telephone number, property address, and other information about the property or your situation. Forms are provided through HubSpot and are also subject to HubSpot’s privacy practices.

How information may be used

We may use submitted information to respond to your request, evaluate whether the property may fit our buying criteria, communicate about the property, maintain business records, improve our website, and comply with legal obligations.

Website and analytics information

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how people find and use this website. Google Analytics may collect information such as device and browser type, general location, referral source, pages viewed, and interactions with contact buttons or forms. This information helps us improve the website and understand which pages lead to inquiries. Google provides information about its privacy practices and user controls in its Privacy Policy.

Sharing

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the website and lead system, and with attorneys, title professionals, contractors, lenders, potential transaction participants, or authorities when reasonably necessary for a requested property review, transaction, or legal obligation.

Calls and text messages

Providing a telephone number allows us to respond about your inquiry. Automated or marketing text messages should only be sent when the required consent has been obtained. Message and data rates may apply. You may ask us to stop marketing communications.

Security and retention

No internet transmission is completely secure. We use reasonable business practices but cannot guarantee absolute security. Information may be retained as reasonably needed for the purposes described above and applicable obligations.

Your choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of information by contacting nchomebuying@gmail.com. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate business or legal reasons.

Seller Guides · Unfinished renovation

Sell a House With an Unfinished Renovation in the NC Triad

If the renovation stopped halfway through, you can get an offer based on the work that’s complete and what’s still open. Photos, a room-by-room scope, and permit or inspection records make it easier to compare finishing the job with selling now.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Make a handoff list by room or system

Separate demolition from completed work. Note whether plumbing, wiring, or HVAC was installed, tested, or inspected. If cabinets, flooring, or other materials already at the property will stay, list them. Keep any unsafe or unfinished rooms off limits during the walkthrough.

Check what would carry into a sale

Ask the local inspections department about open permits and what a new owner would need to do to resume the work. If any contractor balance, notice, or dispute is outstanding, share it with the closing attorney early. Then compare the cost and time to finish with the as-is price. See Toby’s finished Kingston Road renovation for an example of where a completed project can end up.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Scope of work, recent photos, and which utilities are operational
  • Permit or inspection numbers and the last work completed
  • Materials that stay and any outstanding contractor balance to review with your attorney

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

The process

How an as-is home offer works in the Triad.

The short version: you share the property, we review the numbers, and you decide whether the offer is useful.

1

Share the address.

Tell us where the house is, what condition it is in, and what you are hoping to solve.

2

We estimate repairs and resale value.

We look at local comparable sales, visible repair needs, cleanup, carrying costs, and a realistic resale path.

3

You compare your options.

You can accept, decline, ask questions, or decide that a traditional listing is the better path.

OptionMay fit whenTradeoff
As-is cash offerRepairs, timing, privacy, or certainty matter most.The offer is usually below a fully prepared retail sale price.
Traditional listingThe home is show-ready or you have time to prepare it.You may manage repairs, showings, inspections, buyer financing, and longer timelines.
Repair then listYou have the cash, time, and project tolerance to improve the property first.Renovation budgets and timelines can move.

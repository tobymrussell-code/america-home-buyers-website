Compare the house, land and belongings separately

When discussing a Lexington property, describe the house and anything else included: a detached garage, sheds, acreage or a second parcel. Those items can affect access, cleanup and the scope of a direct offer. If the property was inherited, start with the ownership and title questions before setting a closing date.

A road address alone may not identify every parcel you intend to sell. Have parcel information available when possible, and explain whether the family plans to remove furniture, tools or personal items.

Uptown Lexington and the Main–Center Street area

Lexington’s land-use plan identifies historic Uptown around the city square at Main and Center Streets. For a house near Uptown, document the building’s age, usable living area, parking and any additions before comparing offers. Nearby commercial activity does not establish a residential value by itself. Ask which sold properties support the offer and whether their condition resembles yours.

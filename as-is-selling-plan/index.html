Your as-is selling plan

  1. Share the basics

    Send the address. Tell us who can let us in, whether anyone lives there, and which repairs or belongings you already know about.

  2. Review the property with Toby

    We’ll arrange access, look at the condition, and talk about what could stay and when you’d like to close.

  3. Compare the written terms

    If we can make an offer, compare what you’d keep after any seller costs with what you might keep from listing. Check the closing date and any inspection or cleanout terms before you decide.

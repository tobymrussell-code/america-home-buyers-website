Seller Guides · Buyer financing fell through

Sell a House After Buyer Financing Falls Through in NC

Before accepting another offer, have your agent or closing attorney confirm where the first contract stands. Then find out what you can about the reason it fell through so the next sale addresses the issue early.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Work out what the next buyer needs to know

Ask whether the issue involved buyer qualification, appraisal, insurance, property condition, or a lender repair requirement. In some cases, you may only know that the lender didn’t approve the loan. If the sale exposed a roof issue, septic concern, or permit problem, share the reports you have with your agent or attorney and decide how to address it before relisting.

Compare relisting with a direct purchase

If the setback was specific to the buyer, relisting may still make sense. If the house itself created a repeated obstacle, a cash buyer who can take it as-is may be worth comparing. Review proof of funds and written terms, including inspection access, whether the buyer can assign the contract, and when they can terminate or change the price. Compare those terms alongside expected proceeds and the date you need to move.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Property address and whether your prior contract is still active
  • What you were told about the cause, plus any inspection or repair reports you may share
  • Your next deadline and whether the home is already vacant

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

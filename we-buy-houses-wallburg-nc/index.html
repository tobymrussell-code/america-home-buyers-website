No pressure to accept

Use the offer as one number in a real comparison.

Listing can be the better path when a house is market-ready and the owner has time. A direct as-is sale can be useful when the work, privacy, timing, or certainty matters more. Ask what assumptions are built into every offer and what could change before closing.

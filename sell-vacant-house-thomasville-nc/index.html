Sell a Vacant House As-Is in Thomasville, NC

You can ask for an as-is offer on a vacant Thomasville house before turning utilities back on, clearing furniture, or starting repairs. Tell the buyer what’s off, who has access, and what’s changed since the house was last occupied.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

What to check before a walkthrough

Start with the last date someone had regular access. Confirm who has a key and tell the buyer about boarded openings, loose steps, signs of a break-in, or rooms that can’t be entered safely. If the water or electricity is off, say so before the visit. Ask your insurer what your policy requires while the house is vacant.

Compare selling now with keeping it longer

Add up the insurance, taxes, yard care, and any mortgage payments for the time you expect to keep the house. Then estimate the cleanout and repair costs that would be necessary for your listing plan. Compare what you’d keep after those costs with a written as-is offer. See how a vacant Thomasville property sold while the owners lived in Myrtle Beach.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Street address, last occupied date, and who can let us in
  • Which utilities are on and any known damage or access restrictions
  • Furniture, equipment, or belongings you would like to leave

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

Selling a Thomasville house that needs repairs

For a Thomasville property, separate the work needed to make the house safe from the work a retail buyer might prefer. Tell Toby about roof leaks, older electrical or plumbing systems, and belongings that will remain. A vacant house with inaccessible rooms needs a different walkthrough plan from an occupied home.

Photograph the exterior, kitchen, bathrooms and any damaged areas if you can do so safely. Explain whether the property is owner-occupied, rented or vacant, and whether anyone else needs to approve a sale.

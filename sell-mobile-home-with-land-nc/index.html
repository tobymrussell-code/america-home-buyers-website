Seller Guides · Mobile home with land

Sell a Mobile Home With Land in the NC Triad

If you own both the manufactured home and the land, we can review them together. Start with the parcel number and whatever title records you have. How the home is titled affects the paperwork and can affect which buyers can finance it.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Find out how the home and land are recorded

Locate the land deed and the manufactured home title or recorded affidavit, if you have them. A tax bill showing the home is useful for research. Ask the closing attorney to confirm whether there’s a separate title, who owns it, and how any liens should be handled. Review the North Carolina title cancellation statute with the attorney if the home is affixed to the land.

Explain what comes with the property

Tell the buyer which lots are included and whether the driveway, well, or septic serves another parcel. If the home has an added room, deck, or second unit, share what you know about when it was added. If you only own the home and rent the lot, say that up front so the sale can be evaluated on the right terms.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Parcel ID, year, make, and title or serial number if known
  • Whether you own the land and which lots are part of the sale
  • Well, septic, utility connections, shared access, and known damage

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

When a direct sale may help.

An as-is sale may be useful when the property requires more repairs, time, access coordination, or cleanup than the owner wants to manage. It should still be compared with listing.

  • Deferred interior or exterior maintenance.
  • Inherited belongings and cleanout.
  • Vacant or difficult-to-manage property.
  • Rental turnover and tenant complications.

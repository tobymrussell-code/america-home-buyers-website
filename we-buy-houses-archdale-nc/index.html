North Main Street and parcel-specific access

Archdale’s zoning ordinance includes North Main overlay standards and provisions addressing lot frontage and street access. If the selling plan includes splitting land, changing an entrance or converting the property to another use, verify the parcel’s zoning before comparing redevelopment offers. For a straightforward house sale, describe existing access and any shared-driveway arrangements early.

Sell a House With an Old or Leaking Roof in the NC Triad

Before paying for a new roof to sell your house, find out what buyers are reacting to. A roof near the end of its life, an active leak, and water damage inside the house can lead to different prices and different buyer options.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Separate roof age from current damage

Gather the installation date if you have it, photos taken safely from ground level, and any contractor quote. Tell buyers which rooms show staining, whether the stain changes after rain, and what repairs have already been done. If the roof or decking was inspected, ask what the quote includes so offers are based on the same scope.

Compare replacement, as-is listing, and a direct offer

Ask your agent what comparable homes suggest buyers would pay with the roof as it sits. Ask a roofer what replacement would include, including any uncertain decking costs. Compare both with a written as-is offer and review insurance or lender concerns with the appropriate professional. If you already filed a claim, bring in your insurer and closing attorney before agreeing on how proceeds or repair obligations are handled.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Approximate age, material, and when leaks were last noticed
  • Roof report, quotes, invoices, and any ceiling damage you know about
  • Whether insurance is involved and whether the house can be shown safely

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.

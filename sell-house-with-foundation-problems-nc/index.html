Seller Guides · Foundation and crawl space

Sell a House With Foundation Problems in the NC Triad

You can request an as-is review before paying for foundation work. Describe what you can see, such as a sloped floor, wall cracks, or moisture in the crawl space, and share any existing report. An observation and an engineer’s diagnosis carry different weight.

Request an As-Is Offer Call Toby

Describe the symptoms and where they show up

Write down the locations of cracks, sticking doors, soft flooring, or basement moisture. Include when you first noticed them and whether they’ve changed. Share drainage or prior repair records if you have them. If a licensed structural engineer has evaluated the house, send the written findings and any estimate based on those findings.

Ask each buyer what repair assumptions drive the price

One buyer may assume a drainage problem while another prices major structural work. Compare the assumptions and ask what would cause a price change after inspection. If you’re listing the house or signing a contract, ask your agent or attorney how existing reports and known conditions should be shared under North Carolina’s disclosure rules.

What to have ready when you contact us

  • Rooms affected and when the symptoms appeared
  • Structural reports, contractor quotes, and repair or drainage records
  • Any areas that should be kept off limits during a walkthrough

Start with your phone number and the property address. You can talk through the details with Toby when he follows up.
